Her story

She built it all on her own.

For more than 25 years, Sridevi Polisetty has stood tall on her own two feet. She grew up in East Godavari district in a landlord farming family, but chose a path of her own: fashion and tailoring.

  1. East Godavari rootsBorn into a landlord farmers’ family, she left home with a dream of becoming a designer.
  2. Fashion designerShe began her career designing and stitching, learning the craft one garment at a time.
  3. Fashion technician at VEPZWorking in the industry gave her a sharp eye for finishing, accuracy and quality.
  4. Her own tailoring businessShe started this shop in Vadlapudi, Visakhapatnam, and has been growing it ever since.

Today her work travels well beyond Visakhapatnam. Her clients include customers in the USA, UK, Australia, Hyderabad and Bangalore.
